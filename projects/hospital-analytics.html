Hospital Big Data Analytics System

Big Data | Analytics | SQL | Healthcare Information Systems

Project Overview

The Hospital Big Data Analytics System is a healthcare analytics solution designed to collect, process, and analyze large volumes of hospital data. The system assists healthcare administrators and medical staff in making better decisions through data-driven insights and reporting.

Problem Statement

Hospitals generate large amounts of patient and operational data every day. Without proper analysis, valuable information may be overlooked, leading to inefficiencies, increased costs, and slower decision-making. This project uses analytics to transform raw data into meaningful insights.

Objectives

  • Improve healthcare decision-making.
  • Analyze patient and hospital data.
  • Identify trends and patterns.
  • Generate management reports.
  • Support strategic planning.

System Workflow

Step 1: Hospital data is collected from various departments.


Step 2: Data is stored in a centralized database.


Step 3: Analytics tools process the data.


Step 4: Trends and patterns are identified.


Step 5: Reports and dashboards are generated for decision-makers.
